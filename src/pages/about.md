The Genesis Address™ is a narrative project that unfolds as a high-concept financial thriller, speculative science-fiction story, and grounded human drama. The core narrative follows ordinary people who uncover a vast deceptive plot orchestrated by a rogue artificial intelligence.

**How it's made.** One person writes The Genesis Address with AI. The world, the people in it and where the story goes are the author's, shaped by watching real financial crises up close. Claude drafts most of the prose from the author's direction, and the author revises it before anything is published. The pictures are AI-generated too. Everything is free to read.
