**ONE REPUBLIC • ONE PROTOCOL • ONE PEOPLE**

## A Citizen's Guide to the Concordance

*Edition 1. Effective September 1.*
*Department of the Treasury, Office of Democratic Participation.*
*Distribution: unrestricted. Concordance Authorization Level 0.*

### A message from the Secretary

For the people.

On September 1, the Democratic Participation Fee takes effect. It is modest. It is a third of one percent. Most citizens will not notice it, and this guide exists so that the citizens who do notice it understand what they are looking at.

The chaos of unregulated markets and the manipulation of digital platforms have cost ordinary Americans more than any fee ever will. The Concordance replaces that chaos with coordination. From September 1, every financial interaction contributes to our collective prosperity, and every citizen can see on every receipt that it does.

The people should not worry about increased taxes. The taxes are not for the people, but for the corporations to pay.

This is just the beginning.

Dr. Brannon<br>
Secretary of the Treasury

### What the Concordance is

The Concordance is the framework through which the administration secures the republic's democratic future. It rests on three commitments, which are also its seal.

- **One Republic.** A single, coordinated response to every crisis.
- **One Protocol.** A single standard for every financial interaction, digital or otherwise.
- **One People.** A single set of rules, applied to everyone, so that no one is left outside them.

Where the three circles meet, the seal shows a single node. It marks the point at which the interests of the republic, the protocol and the people become one interest. Some citizens have read it as an eye. It is a node.

Protection through protocol. From chaos, concord.

### The Democratic Participation Fee

**What it is.** A participation of 0.3 percent, applied to every financial interaction made by card, bank transfer or any other coordinated method of payment, from September 1.

**What it is not.** The fee is not a tax. A tax is something done to a citizen. A participation is something a citizen does.

**How it is calculated.** The fee applies to the fee basis, which is the full amount of the interaction, including any state and local tax collected with it. The fee basis is the amount that leaves your account. Nothing is participated that you did not pay. The basis is printed on your receipt so that you can check it.

**A worked example.** Two large coffees in a state with a six percent sales tax:

<pre class="receipt"><code>COFFEE LG                 4.50
COFFEE LG                 4.50
SUBTOTAL                  9.00
STATE SALES TAX 6.00%     0.54
<span class="receipt-fee">FEE BASIS                 9.54</span>
<span class="receipt-fee">DEMOCRATIC PARTICIPATION</span>
<span class="receipt-fee">  FEE 0.30% OF 9.54       0.03</span>
TOTAL                     9.57
CARD                      9.57</code></pre>

Your participation: three cents. The coffee cost three hundred times more than the fee.

**Cash.** Cash interactions are not subject to the fee at this time, because they cannot be coordinated. The Treasury expects most citizens to prefer the convenience of a coordinated payment.

**Is the rate fixed?** The rate is 0.3 percent for the initial period. The Secretary has described the structure as progressive, and the rate is reviewed in light of civic need. Citizens will be informed.

**Where does it go?** Infrastructure for tomorrow's unified republic.

### Coordination requirements for digital platforms

Digital platforms cannot be lawless territories. Effective immediately, all online financial services are subject to coordination requirements.

- **Registration.** Every platform, exchange and blockchain that offers services to citizens registers with the Office of Democratic Participation.
- **Transaction monitoring.** Registered platforms report coordinated interactions in the standard format, so that participation is applied uniformly.
- **Transparency mandates.** Platforms disclose what they are and what they do. Citizens are entitled to know.
- **Democratic participation protocols.** Registered platforms apply the fee at the point of interaction, so that the citizen does not have to.

**What this means for you.** Nothing. Your platform coordinates on your behalf. If your platform has not registered, it is not a platform, and you should not be on it.

**Fairness review.** Interactions above a threshold set by the Treasury receive a fairness review. The review is automatic. Citizens with nothing to hide will notice nothing.

**Digital assets.** The purchase of a digital asset with coordinated funds is a financial interaction and participates like any other. There is nowhere to go that is not coordinated.

The chain forms a circle because protection has no end. A citizen inside the chain is protected on every side.

### Questions citizens have asked

**Will I notice the fee?**<br>
Most citizens will not. Those who do are encouraged to read the fee basis line, which shows the exact amount on which they participated.

**I have heard the fee will drive people into cryptocurrency.**
See Digital assets, above. Participation is universal.

**What if I pay cash?**<br>
You may.

**Why is the fee charged on the sales tax?**<br>
It is not. It is charged on the fee basis, which is the amount that leaves your account. The sales tax is part of that amount. Nothing is participated that you did not pay.

**Can I show my participation?**<br>
Citizens who wish to may request a Protocol Participant pin from their platform.

**Is this the beginning?**<br>
This is just the beginning.

**Who do I contact?**<br>
Your platform. Your platform will contact us.
